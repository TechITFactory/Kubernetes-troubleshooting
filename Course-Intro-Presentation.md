### Slide 3: The "Break-Fix" Strategy
**Visuals:**
- **Traditional Learning:** How to build.
- **Our Strategy:** How to **Fix**.
- **The Method:** Intentionally trigger production-grade failures.

**Speaker Script:**
"Most courses show you how to build a cluster from scratch. In this course, we do something different. We use a unique 'Break-Fix' strategy.
